The party store Utica has trusted since 1956.

Buscemi's Party Shoppe shares a roof with the original Buscemi's pizza and sub shop at 8315 Hall Rd, just off M-59 and minutes from Sterling Heights, Shelby Township, and Clinton Township. Pizza on one side, a full party store on the other. That is how it has worked here for three generations.
